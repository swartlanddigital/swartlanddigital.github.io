ApplyRight SA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who operates ApplyRight SA

CV Maker SA: ApplyRight (also called ApplyRight SA) is published on Google Play by SDZA. Privacy questions can be sent to info@swartlanddigital.co.za.

What the app does

ApplyRight SA helps people prepare CVs and cover letters, track job applications, maintain a document checklist and practise interview questions. It is not an employer, recruiter or government service and does not guarantee employment.

Information kept on the device

Your employment profile, CV content, application records, checklist state and generated documents are stored locally on your device. A portrait is accessed only after you select it through Android’s system picker. The app does not scan unrelated photos or files.

CV and cover-letter PDFs are generated locally. They leave your device only when you deliberately save or share them. ApplyRight disables Android app-data backup for its locally stored profile, tracker and checklist data.

Optional account information

Signing in does not upload, synchronise or back up your CVs, applications or letters. Device data and your authentication account have separate deletion controls.

You may continue as a guest. If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes your name, email address, Firebase user ID, email-verification and sign-in status, and technical security information such as IP address, app identifier, SDK/platform information and user-agent information. If you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies profile information covered by the permissions shown to you.

Passwords are transmitted securely to Firebase Authentication and are not stored in ApplyRight’s local application data. Account data is encrypted in transit.

Advertising and consent

The free app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform. When advertising services operate, they process an IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Your consent choices and device settings affect advertising personalisation and the identifiers available; non-personalised ads may still process data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Where required, the app presents Google’s consent form before requesting ads. You can reopen advertising privacy choices inside the app. Android advertising identifiers can also be reset or deleted in Android settings.

Why information is used

  • Provide CV, cover-letter, application-tracking and interview-practice features.
  • Create, authenticate and manage optional accounts.
  • Send verification and password-reset emails you request.
  • Protect services against fraud and abuse.
  • Display and measure advertising that funds the free app.
  • Diagnose service and app-performance problems.

ApplyRight does not sell the CV or application information you enter locally.

Service providers

ApplyRight uses Google services including Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, Google sign-in, Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform. Their processing is governed by Google’s applicable terms and privacy notices. If you share a document with another app, that app’s privacy practices apply.

Retention and deletion

Local profile, application and checklist data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Exported documents remain wherever you saved or shared them.

Signed-in users can delete their authentication account from the Account screen. You can also use the ApplyRight SA account-deletion page. Firebase may retain limited information where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or service integrity. Account deletion does not remove files you previously exported or shared.

V2 data controls

Manual backup and restore

You can save a JSON backup through Android's document picker. It contains CV versions, contact details, applications, job adverts, letters and checklist progress. ApplyRight does not encrypt this file; anyone with access may read it. Choose a private location. A cloud-backed destination may upload/store it under that provider's terms. Portraits, exported PDFs and sign-in credentials are excluded. Restore replaces current records after confirmation, not a merge; portraits must be selected again. Automatic app-data backup is disabled and backup/transfer exclusion rules are included, although device-provider behaviour may vary.

Local follow-up reminders

Reminders are optional and off by default. Their enabled setting, chosen local time and last notification date stay on the device. Due application dates drive generic local notifications, not server push messages. Notification permission is requested where required; manage reminders in More or Android settings. Android may delay delivery.

Rewarded exports and optional reviews

Editing and PDF preview are free. Saving/sharing unlocks when the chosen rewarded ad sequence confirms completion; a sequence may contain multiple ads. Document text is not sent to the ad provider for this unlock. Editing creates a new export; unlocks last only for the current app session.

An on-device counter tracks up to two successful PDF saves and whether a review request was attempted, without CV content, filenames, account identifiers or ratings. The release app may then request Google's native review flow; debug phone-test builds do not. Google controls whether it appears. The app does not receive your rating/review text through this flow and offers no reward for a review. Google handles submitted reviews under its terms; they may be public or private developer feedback in closed testing. You can delete a review through your Google Play/Google account. No separate remote product-analytics service is used for this counter. See Google's review data information.

Removing local data

More > Delete all local app data removes saved CV versions, applications, letters, checklist progress, the local review counter and private generated-document cache, releases selected-file permissions and disables reminders. It does not delete original photos, external PDFs/JSON backups, shared copies or your Firebase account. Manage external copies with their storage/app provider. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local preferences and may lose unexported work.

Security

The app uses Android platform controls, Firebase App Check and encrypted network connections provided by its service providers. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Age eligibility and younger users

ApplyRight SA is intended for people aged 16 and over preparing CVs and job applications. It is not directed to anyone under 16. If you are 16 or 17, involve a parent or guardian where their permission is required by applicable law or the terms of a service you use. Do not enter another person's personal information without their permission. A parent or guardian with questions about a younger user's information can contact info@swartlanddigital.co.za; account and local-data deletion options are described above.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The latest public version will display its revision date.
