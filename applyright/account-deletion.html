Delete your ApplyRight SA account

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

For CV Maker SA: ApplyRight (ApplyRight SA), published on Google Play by SDZA

Delete inside the app

  1. Open ApplyRight SA and select More > Account and sign-in.
  2. Sign in if needed.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.
  4. If Firebase asks for a recent sign-in, sign out, sign in again and repeat the deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email the address below from the same email address used for your ApplyRight account. Use the subject ApplyRight account deletion. Support may ask you to verify ownership without asking for your password.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

The Firebase Authentication account and its name, email and user identifier are deleted. ApplyRight currently does not upload your CV, cover-letter, application-tracker or checklist content to an ApplyRight database.

What remains

Signing in does not back up or sync your CVs. Account deletion and local-data deletion are separate. Use More > Delete all local app data to remove CV versions, applications, letters, checklist progress, the local review counter and private generated-document cache. This releases selected-photo access and disables reminders; it does not delete the original photo or a saved JSON backup. Anyone with a backup file may still read it: delete unwanted external copies using their storage provider. Reviews you submitted to Google Play are managed through your Google Play/Google account.

Deleting an account does not remove data stored only on a device, exported PDFs, documents you shared with other apps, or information retained where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations. To remove local app data, use Delete all local data inside ApplyRight or clear the app’s storage in Android settings.
